# 🏥 Smart Hospital Appointment & Patient Management System

##  Project Overview

This project is a Business Analyst case study based on a real-world healthcare scenario. The objective of this project is to transform the manual hospital appointment and patient management process into a digital system.

The project covers the complete Business Analysis lifecycle, starting from understanding the business problem, gathering requirements, documenting the solution, preparing process diagrams, validating the requirements, analyzing hospital data using SQL, and building an interactive Power BI dashboard for business reporting.

---

#  Business Problem

The hospital was managing patient appointments, registrations, and records manually using Excel sheets and paper registers. This resulted in:

- Long patient waiting time
- Appointment conflicts
- Duplicate patient records
- Manual reporting
- Difficult access to patient information

The hospital wanted a centralized web-based system to improve operational efficiency and provide better patient service.

---

#  Proposed Solution

A Smart Hospital Appointment & Patient Management System that allows:

- Online patient registration
- Appointment booking and scheduling
- Doctor schedule management
- Centralized patient records
- Hospital performance reporting
- Management dashboards using Power BI
